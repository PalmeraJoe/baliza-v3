# Turbidity and optics

Allen publishes an annual turbidity layer in FNU, with download values scaled by 10 and a stated cap of 100 FNU (`sources/allen-coral-atlas.md`).

EMODnet Physics lists light attenuation as a parameter. No reef product was selected.

SRC-HUGHES-2017 reported minimal effect of water quality on the severity of 2016 GBR bleaching. That does not establish that turbidity never matters, and it does not establish an effect size. No optical threshold is adopted.
