# Evidence gaps

Recorded, not filled:

- No local bleaching, cover, species, depth, or disease series.
- No CRS confirmation for the NOAA NetCDF or the Allen tiles.
- No reef-by-reef inventory of EMODnet Physics.
- Aspect not confirmed.
- No adopted definition of stress duration, frequency, or onset.
- Acclimatization not established; one GBR result argues against assuming protection from prior bleaching.
- No opened IPCC chapter in this pass.
- Disease rates not quantified.
- Downscaling skill unknown. The exact temperature target stays OPEN.
- Playbook entry conditions deliberately absent.
- Reef Data Fabric is specified and not built. CRS, licence, and training permission are still open.
- No resort historical dataset has been ingested. Absence of that history is a cold start, not a measured zero, and not "no bleaching".
- MERMAID does not fill every ecological gap. Disease was not found. Richness is unverified. Cover and bleaching exist only for submitted samples and allowed policies. Missing MERMAID rows are not zeros.
