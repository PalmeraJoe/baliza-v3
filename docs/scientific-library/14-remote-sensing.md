# Remote sensing

Two families are in scope:

- NOAA CRW v3.1 thermal grids, daily, 0.05°, 1985–present. External observations.
- Allen Coral Atlas v2 optical maps, 5 m habitat and 10 m depth, epoch about 2018–2020. Context.

Neither family is ground truth. The CRW outlook product is a prediction and stays off the observation path. Details: `sources/noaa-crw.md` and `sources/allen-coral-atlas.md`.
