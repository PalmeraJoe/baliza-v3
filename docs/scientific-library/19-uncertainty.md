# Uncertainty

Phase 6 uncertainty types stay qualitative: known, unknown, insufficient, conflicting, suspect.

For these sources, additional uncertainty that must stay visible:

- Surface satellite temperature versus colony temperature.
- 5 km pixel versus transect.
- Allen accuracy 60–90% by region.
- Annual turbidity versus survey day.
- CRW climatology version.
- Empty local ground truth.

No confidence fraction is assigned.
