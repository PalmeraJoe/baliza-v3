# Coral bleaching fundamentals

Mass bleaching, in the sources opened here, is the breakdown of the coral–symbiont relationship under environmental stress, notably elevated sea temperature (SRC-HOEGH-1999). Bleached tissue is not automatically dead. NOAA's methodology page says prolonged bleaching often leads to death and that recovery of a severely affected reef can take decades; that page cites other works for those sentences.

SRC-HUGHES-2017 reports that the geographic pattern of recurrent GBR bleaching followed the pattern of sea temperature, and that in 2016 water quality and fishing pressure had minimal effect on severity. That finding is about that study. It is not a licence to ignore local context everywhere.

No BALIZA threshold follows from these papers.
