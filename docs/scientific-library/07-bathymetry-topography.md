# Bathymetry and topography

EMODnet 2024 DTM: 1/16 arc minute on the European seas listed in `sources/emodnet.md`.

Allen: 10 m satellite-derived depth where the optical method applies.

Slope is a derivative mentioned in Allen mapping. Aspect was not confirmed as a delivered product (`UNKNOWN`).

A 5 km thermal pixel does not have one slope. No topographic index is adopted as a bleaching predictor.
